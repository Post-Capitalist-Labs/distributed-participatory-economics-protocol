## 1. Summary of Major Structural and Substantive Updates in the Working Draft

1. **Claims Downgraded from "Proven / Guaranteed" to Hypotheses & Research Targets:**
   - Retracted the claim in Chapter 4 (Section 4.4.3) that "Algorithm 3" guarantees convergence under Leontief complementarities, non-convexities, and endogenous income feedback.
   - Renamed Chapter 1's Table 1.1 column from *"Demonstrably Achievable Today (Proven by Math & Systems Engineering)"* to ***"Three-Tier Evidence Classification: (A) Empirically Demonstrated, (B) Analytical Estimates, and (C) Research Targets"***.
   - Replaced the initial recommendation (*"PROCEED immediately with the full 16-week integrated PoC"*) with a **staged, kill-gated three-phase experimental programme** (Phase 1: Python-first Economic Coordination & Non-Convexity Kill Gate + Phase 1H Low-Tech Human Participation Probe $\to$ Phase 2: Distributed Coordination & Anti-Censorship $\to$ Phase 3: Integrated Mobile, PQC & `Prio3` Privacy Pilot).
2. **Mathematical & Economic Model Corrections (Chapter 4 & `research/MATHEMATICAL_REVIEW.md`):**
   - Corrected the `pequod-cljs` "toothache" analysis (`R-ECON-06`): proved that $\sum b_m + c/k \in [1.0712, 1.6652] > 1$ across all 150,000 WC records, making the unconstrained WC objective unbounded above and the closed-form FOC a perverse-supply saddle point.
   - Separated **Proximal Damped Tâtonnement** (Algorithm 3a) from **Convex Proximal-Jacobi ADMM** (Algorithm 3b) and **Two-Tier Hierarchical MILP + ADMM Decomposition** (Algorithm 3c), and evaluated all 9 optimisation families.
   - Added Scarf's (1960) 3-good Leontief instability counterexample, the Sonnenschein–Mantel–Debreu theorem, and the Shapley–Folkman–Starr theorem (demonstrating why $K \ge 20$ small plants smooth out non-convex jumps while single indivisible plants require Federation MILP).
   - Fixed the endogenous-income scale indeterminacy (`R-ECON-05`) by introducing an explicit **Numéraire Normalisation Invariant** ($w_{\text{base}} \equiv 1.0$) and closed the Society Account equation (`R-ECON-07`) by dividing by effective effort-weighted hours $\sum_u h_u \epsilon_u$ and explicitly reconciling CAP compensation and net borrowing.
   - Replaced the two-sided $\rho_i \le \tau$ check and exterior ecological penalty with a **strict one-sided feasibility criterion** ($D_g \le S_g$ for goods; $D_r \le r^*$ and $D_z \le E_z^{\max}$ for ecological caps) and an infeasibility detector when baseline needs conflict with biophysical ceilings (`R-ECON-10`).
   - Added explicit engagement with **Janos Kornai** (soft budget constraints), **Martin Weitzman** (ratchet effect & prices vs. quantities), and **Friedrich Hayek** (`R-ECON-08`), plus analysis of workplace $SB/SC$ effort caps vs. Maxim 3 (`R-ECON-09`).
3. **Cryptographic & Distributed Systems Revisions (Chapters 5, 6, 7 & `research/SECURITY_REVIEW.md`):**
   - Fixed the critical unbound budget-proof flaw (`R-SEC-01`) and offline dropout fragility (`R-SEC-02`) by adopting **IETF CFRG `Prio3` / VDAF (`draft-irtf-cfrg-vdaf-16`)** with Fully Linear Proofs over $K=3$ federated aggregators as the primary privacy architecture.
   - Incorporated **Crites & Stewart (IACR ePrint 2025/2046)** on the refutation of up-to-capacity Reed–Solomon proximity-gap conjectures for FRI/STARKs (`R-SEC-05`).
   - Replaced the $1.28\text{ MB}$ naive Sparse Merkle-Sum inclusion path with a **Two-Root Authenticated Aggregation Structure** ($512\text{ B}$ standard BLAKE3 inclusion path + full leaf data availability across `Role-SR` nodes + active/partitioned council bitmaps + signed relay receipts and challenge windows; `R-SEC-06`).
   - Upgraded the root anchor to **`SLH-DSA-SHA2-192s` (NIST FIPS 205 Level 3)** for uniform Level-3 security (`R-SEC-10`), specified **RFC 8949 Canonical CBOR** with fixed scale $10^{-6}$ and 128-bit intermediate rounding (`R-SEC-08`), corrected mobile round bandwidth to **`250–400 KB/round`** (`R-TECH-02`), and fixed the Table 6.1 weighted architecture scores with a full sensitivity analysis (`R-TECH-05`).
4. **Historical, Institutional, Political & Transition Revisions (Chapters 2, 8, 9, 10, 11):**
   - Replaced the paraphrased Chapter 2 epigraph with Gaston Leval's verbatim text from Dolgoff (1974, p. 6 / *Espagne Libertaire*, p. 11) (`R-SRC-01`).
   - Incorporated **Michael Seidman (1991, 2002)** and **Julián Casanova (2005)** on wartime coercion, smallholder conflict, worker resistance to work discipline, and Líster's August 1937 armed dissolution of the Council of Aragon (`R-HIST-01`), as well as **Finley (1981)** and **Ste. Croix (1981)** on slavery and Delian League tribute in Classical Athens.
   - Corrected Mondragón's *Consejo Social* vs. *Comisión de Vigilancia* / Audit Committee and *socios* share (`R-SRC-02`), Porto Alegre's 2017 suspension (`R-SRC-03`), Rojava's January 30, 2026 SDF–Damascus integration accord (`R-SRC-04`), Hurwicz (1972) (`R-SRC-05`), and Freeman (1972) vs. Mansbridge (1980) / Dahl & Tufte (1973) (`R-SRC-06`).
   - Added substantial responses to **Jan Philipp Dapprich (2024)**, **Seth Ackerman (2014)**, and **Paul Cockshott & Allin Cottrell (1993)** (`R-POL-04`), constitutional governance of protocol parameters (`R-POL-01`), hardware-decoupled sortition (`R-POL-02`), civil-liberties safeguards (`R-POL-06`), environmental justice in CAPs (`R-POL-07`), and a comprehensive **Chapter 11 on Cooperative Adoption and Transition Theory** (`R-POL-08`).
