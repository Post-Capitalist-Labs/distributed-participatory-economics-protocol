## Classification Taxonomy

Every major scientific, mathematical, economic, cryptographic, and engineering claim in the monograph is classified into:
- **Classification:** `Established` (peer-reviewed theorem or standard) / `Source-supported` (directly verified in primary literature or audited code) / `Analytical` (derived from formal message/complexity models) / `Conjectural` (theoretical hypothesis) / `Experimental target` (engineering goal to be tested).
- **Status:** `Verified` / `Partially verified` / `Unverified` / `Refuted (Corrected)`.
