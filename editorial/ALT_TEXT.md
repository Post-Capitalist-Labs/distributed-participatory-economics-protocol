# Alt Text and Long Descriptions (draft, for author review)

These follow University of Chicago Press guidance: alt text of 150 characters or fewer that describes rather than explains, plus a long description where the figure carries information the alt text cannot. They are drafted from each figure's TikZ source and caption. Not yet inserted into the book: LaTeX PDF tagging (for example the `tagpdf` package, or a later EPUB conversion) is a production decision.
