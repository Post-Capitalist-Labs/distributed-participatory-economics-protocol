**General points for the publisher:**

1. **Epigraphs.** Many publishers treat an epigraph as decorative, so the fair-dealing defence for criticism and review is weak for epigraphs and permission is usually sought whatever the length. Nine of the eleven epigraphs come from two Albert and Hahnel books.
2. **Open licence.** The book is released under CC BY-SA 4.0. The copyright page excludes quoted material from the licence, which is correct. A rights holder granting permission should be told that the book is openly licensed. Some grant permission only for print, or only for non-open editions.
3. **Cumulative use.** Albert and Hahnel's *The Political Economy of Participatory Economics* (Princeton University Press, 1991) and *Looking Forward* (South End Press, 1991) are quoted in epigraphs and repeatedly in the text. Cumulative extent matters for fair dealing and fair use.
